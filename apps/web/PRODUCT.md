# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Duoji serves individuals and small groups, including friends, partners, roommates, and travel companions. They record income and expenses, split costs, confirm who paid, settle balances, and review reports during everyday spending, shared living, meals, and trips.

Their core job is to capture accurate financial information with minimal cognitive load while keeping every group member clear about shared spending and settlement outcomes.

## Product Purpose

Duoji combines personal expense tracking, group cost splitting, shared account books, settlements, and reporting in one web application. It reduces the memory, calculation, and communication overhead of shared spending.

A successful experience lets users record expenses quickly, trust amounts and split results, and retain clear context across account books, members, and spending situations.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, and light.

Duoji feels approachable, reassuring, and easy to start. It carries the pleasant atmosphere of travel and shared life without allowing decoration to interfere with amounts, states, or primary actions. Its voice is clear and direct without blame or financial anxiety.

## Anti-references

- Do not use cold, severe, or overly authoritative corporate-finance interfaces.
- Do not use black, saturated neon, or dense information to imitate a technical trading terminal.
- Do not rely on pervasive glass blur, decorative gradients, excessive shadows, or animation that communicates no state.
- Do not reinvent familiar controls for style or give the same action inconsistent visual treatments across pages.
- Do not let illustrations, brand colors, or motion reduce the readability of amounts, split results, errors, or action states.

## Design Principles

1. **Complete the task before expressing the brand**: amounts, dates, payers, split details, and primary actions always precede decoration.
2. **Shared finances must earn trust**: states, calculations, and available actions stay explicit instead of requiring interpretation.
3. **Familiar controls, consistent language**: the same function uses the same component, label, and interaction across pages.
4. **Keep group relationships relaxed**: copy and state presentation stay neutral and friendly, reducing the stress of tracking and requesting money.
5. **Remain calm in mobile contexts**: core work remains possible on small screens, with one-handed use, long text, and network or asset failures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA at minimum: normal text contrast is at least 4.5:1, while large text and non-text interactive boundaries are at least 3:1.
- Never communicate an interactive state through color alone; pair it with an icon, text, shape, or readable label.
- Support keyboard navigation, clear focus indicators, and primary touch targets of at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing non-essential translation and scaling.
- Support widths down to 320px, device safe areas, and long English or Traditional Chinese content without obscuring amounts or actions.
